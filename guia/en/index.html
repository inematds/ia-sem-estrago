How it works

Two safeguards, no jargon

The kit doesn’t make AI smarter. It puts two limits around it, so you can step away.

🔒 1. The guard

It works like a locked room: AI can move around the house but can’t go in there. To decide what stays locked, the kit asks one question: “If AI gets this wrong once, can you undo it?”

If you can undo it (edit text or change code that Git tracks), it’s allowed.
If you can’t (delete a whole folder, read passwords, or erase the project history), it stays locked in every mode, even if AI tries.

✅ 2. The test before “done”

It works like an inspector at the end of a production line. You say once what “done” means, and a test checks every time AI tries to finish.

It passes: AI delivers the work.
It fails: AI gets the reason, goes back, and fixes it. To prevent an endless loop, it stops and calls you after 3 tries.

🤝 What about everything else?

Actions you can undo happen on their own, without asking at every step. Claude Code’s automatic mode decides. If AI makes a mistake, you can undo it. The kit doesn’t promise AI will never make a mistake. It promises that mistakes you can’t fix won’t happen, and that “done” means done.
